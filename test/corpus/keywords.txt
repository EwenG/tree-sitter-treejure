==================
Basic Keyword
==================
:foo
---
(source
  (keyword
    name: (symbol_name)))

==================
Namespaced Keyword (Multi-slash)
==================
:ee/rr/tt
---
(source
  (keyword
    namespace: (symbol_name)
    name: (symbol_name)))

==================
Auto-resolved Keyword
==================
::foo
---
(source
  (keyword
    name: (symbol_name)))

==================
Auto-resolved Namespaced
==================
::my-ns/foo
---
(source
  (keyword
    namespace: (symbol_name)
    name: (symbol_name)))
